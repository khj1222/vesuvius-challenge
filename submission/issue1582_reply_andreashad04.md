Thanks, @AndreasHad04. This is the measurement my own write-up said was missing. When I tested the family explanation on PHerc0139, the reservation I could not remove was that all five native representations in the corpus belong to that one scroll, so a gap measured there might be a property of 0139. A native volume of a scroll outside the corpus, with the same labels and the same physical segments, is the way to settle that. 42 of 42 settles it as far as direction goes: the pooled input is ahead off 0139 too, and it is ahead with the released checkpoints, not only with my retrained ones.

One point on the reading. "The family that matches most of the training corpus is ahead" is how I first read my own result, and a pre-registered arm went against it. I held w035 and w039 out of both families while keeping native w040, w041 and w044 in training, which put native in 16.4% of training batches instead of 0%. The aligned-over-native gap on the held-out pair moved from +0.061 to +0.058. On that evidence the cause is the input itself: a prepared aligned voxel averages 64 acquired voxels, where a native voxel is one acquisition (the pooling measurement earlier in this thread). Your PHerc0841 numbers fit either explanation, because the released checkpoints saw both families. So I'd count them as confirming the gap on a new scroll, not as separating the two causes. Write-up: [docs/15, appendix 2](https://github.com/khj1222/vesuvius-challenge/blob/main/docs/15_loso_cross_scroll.md).

On First Letters, one practical point that fits your depth-direction finding in #1867. When I rendered the eligible native segments myself with `vc_render_tifxyz`, the default orientation came out with the slice order reversed relative to the team's published surface volumes. On a labelled control that orientation turned every checkpoint into the trivial "all ink" classifier. `--flip-normals` reproduced the team's volume (99.74% of bytes). Details are in [docs/25 §8.1](https://github.com/khj1222/vesuvius-challenge/blob/main/docs/25_scouting_eligible_volumes.md).
